# Privacy Policy — MyMoney™ Last updated: November 4, 2026 MyMoney app — Android package ID: com.anspro.mymoney.app ## 1. Identity and scope This Privacy Policy describes the MyMoney™ mobile app, which may also be displayed as MeuDinheiro or MiDinero depending on the selected language. The app is developed by Anselmo Eller and uses the Android package identifier com.anspro.mymoney.app. MyMoney is a personal organization tool. It helps you record and review financial and everyday information, but it is not a bank, financial institution, payment provider, investment platform, or bank account aggregation service. ## 2. Information you enter Depending on the features you use, the app accesses and stores information you choose to enter on your own device, including: - Financial Management: salary or monthly reference amounts, expenses, bills, due dates, payment status, installments and recurring expenses. - My Income: income sources, expected and received amounts, dates, descriptions and notes. - Personal Notes: titles and written content. - Task List: titles, descriptions and completion status. - My Story (Pro): dated journal entries; MyMoney does not store audio files. - My Savings Plan (Pro): savings goal, saved amounts and contribution history. - Preferences: language, country, currency, appearance, notification settings and local profile identifiers. - Access security: optional eight-digit PIN, remembered PIN if selected, and a local recovery code. Avoid entering banking passwords, full card numbers or other secrets into free-form notes. You decide which information to record. ## 3. How the information is used The app uses your entries to display records, organize bills, calculate totals and balances based on amounts you enter, produce charts, search content, track tasks and income, separate language/country/currency profiles and schedule local due-date reminders. Amounts are converted between profiles only when you request it, using a rate you provide. The app does not automatically connect to banks, obtain statements or retrieve live exchange rates. ## 4. On-device storage and data transfers In the reviewed version, financial, income, note and task entries are stored on your device. The app does not require an online account and does not synchronize these records with a developer-operated server. The current app code does not include identified first-party mechanisms that automatically transmit these records to Anselmo Eller, behavioral advertising, or user-behavior analytics. Google Play, operating system services and external websites operate under their own policies. Information processed solely on your device is not, for that reason alone, sent to the developer. If future releases add external transfers or integrations, this policy and the store disclosures will be updated. ## 5. Security and encryption Local databases containing expenses, income, notes and tasks are protected with AES-256-GCM encryption. The encryption key is stored separately using security facilities provided by the operating system. When you enable biometric sign-in in Settings, the operating system verifies your identity, not MyMoney. The app does not collect, store, or transmit fingerprint images or biometric templates. Biometric sign-in can only be enabled after you set a PIN, and PIN sign-in remains available as a fallback. The optional eight-digit PIN restricts access to the app and is required to create or restore compatible backups. Database encryption is active whether or not you enable PIN access. The PIN and recovery code are stored in a protected area on the device. General settings such as theme and language may not use the same encryption as the databases. Security cannot eliminate every risk: compromised devices, access to an unlocked phone, information shown on-screen and visible notifications can expose content. No security measure is infallible. ## 6. Notifications and permissions If you enable reminders, MyMoney may request permission to display device notifications. Due-date reminders are scheduled locally and may display a bill name and due-date information on the screen or in the device notification center. You can change or disable reminders in the app Settings and revoke permission in your device Settings. Notification delivery and visibility are controlled by the operating system. **Microphone use in My Story:** tapping the microphone requests speech-recognition and microphone permissions. Recognized text is saved to the protected profile database, without keeping an audio recording. Depending on the device and operating system voice service, speech samples may be processed by an external provider and require internet. The provider’s privacy terms and settings govern that processing. You can always type without enabling the microphone. **Camera and images in Personal Notes:** when you choose Scan a note, the app requests camera access or opens the system image picker. Text recognition runs on your device. Only text you choose to save becomes part of the note; photos are not attached to the database. The camera or picker may create temporary files managed by the operating system. No photo is sent to an online OCR service. **Exchange rates on the home screen:** while online, the app requests public reference exchange rates from the Frankfurter API, using only the currency codes needed for the lookup. No account values, notes, or personal financial records are sent in these requests. Offline or without a valid rate, the card is hidden. ## 7. Data sharing and third-party services The reviewed app version has no feature that automatically shares your financial and personal entries with the developer, advertisers or social networks. When you follow external links, such as the YouTube channel or this policy webpage, the browser or external service may handle technical access information under its own privacy policy. Opening such a link does not mean MyMoney automatically uploads your financial records. This does not imply that Android, iOS, Google Play or independently visited external providers perform no processing of their own. ## 8. Profiles, retention and deletion Records remain in local storage for as long as you keep them in the app and that storage remains available. Each language, country and currency combination can have a separate database. You can delete selected categories in Settings → Clear Data. Deletion applies to the selected categories in the active profile; other profiles, your PIN and general preferences are not necessarily removed. To remove remaining app data, use the operating system’s storage/removal controls, bearing in mind that system-managed copies may follow other rules. The app does not provide online accounts or a remote account-deletion service. As the entries are stored on your device, the developer cannot remotely inspect, recover or erase them. My Story and My Savings Plan can be erased separately through Clear Data in the active profile. Selecting all categories also removes both areas. ## 9. Backups and risk of data loss The current Android configuration disables automatic app-data backup. There is no online synchronization. Creating backups and performing additional restores are MyMoney Pro features. One free import of an existing backup is available per installation, including during initial setup. Backups made in this version use an eight-digit PIN. Files from older versions using a different password format may be incompatible. Keep both the file and password safe. Uninstalling the app, clearing its data, changing devices or losing the encryption key may make records permanently inaccessible. The recovery code can reset your sign-in PIN; it cannot restore deleted financial databases or recover a lost database encryption key. System backups, restoration and secure storage behavior may vary by platform and operating system version. ## 10. Children and teenagers MyMoney is designed as a general-purpose personal finance and task organization tool and is not specifically directed at children. It is not designed to request data from children or provide regulated financial services. Parents and guardians should assess its suitability for minors. ## 11. Your privacy rights and requests You control the records you enter and can review, edit or delete categories using the app’s features. Because the entries remain on your device and are not accessible to the developer, remote recovery or erasure requests for those records may not be technically possible. We will explain transparently which information is or is not under our control. ## 12.
