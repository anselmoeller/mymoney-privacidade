Privacy Policy

How MyMoney 1.0.8 uses, protects, and lets you control your data.

Last updated: November 8, 2026
MyMoney™ 1.0.8 · Android: com.anspro.mymoney.app
Developer: Anselmo Eller

1. App and purpose

This policy applies to MyMoney™ (also shown as MeuDinheiro or MiDinero), developed by Anselmo Eller, Android package com.anspro.mymoney.app, version 1.0.8. The app helps users organize personal expenses, income, notes, voice recordings, tasks, journals, and savings plans. It is not a bank, brokerage, or bank payment service.

Core personal records are stored locally. No online developer account is required to create or store them.

2. Information you enter

Depending on the features you use, MyMoney stores financial amounts and dates, payment status, income sources, tasks and reminders, note text, journal entries, savings goals, audio recordings, text recognized from photos, and language, region, currency, appearance, and access preferences.

The optional sign-in PIN must be 8-to-16-digit. A registered PIN is also required for protected backup operations and entry to the audio management screen. Please do not store banking passwords or complete card numbers in notes.

3. Local storage and security

Structured profile records, including financial entries, income, text notes, and tasks, are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM. Encryption keys are kept in operating-system-protected secure storage. Some interface preferences use different storage protections.

Recorded audio files are held in the app-private area but are not automatically encrypted in the same way as structured databases. PIN and recovery code protection and biometric authentication rely on device security mechanisms. MyMoney does not collect or store fingerprint templates.

No security control eliminates all risk, particularly on modified or unprotected devices.

4. Voice, transcription, camera, and OCR

With microphone permission, you can record voice notes locally. Speech recognition and dictation may use the device operating system or third-party speech services, which may process audio over a network depending on device configuration. Availability varies by Android/iOS version, language, and device.

When you take or select a photo to scan text, OCR runs on your device. The app does not attach the original image to the saved note, and attempts to remove its temporary image files; the OS or other apps may retain their own copies. Only text you decide to save becomes a note.

You can select and delete multiple text or audio notes. Deleting local records does not delete copies you previously shared or exported.

5. Backups and exports

The developer does not offer automatic cloud backups, and Android automatic app-data backup is disabled. Current version-3 data backups use AES-256-GCM encryption with a scrypt-derived key based on your PIN; they do not include audio recordings. Compatible earlier version-1/2 backup formats can still be imported.

Audio can be exported separately as PIN-encrypted .myaudio files or .myaudiopack bundles. Restore the matching data backup before importing the audio archive. Directly shared audio and journal TXT exports are not automatically protected by the app database encryption.

You choose the destination for exported files. Uninstalling the app, clearing app storage, losing your PIN or losing your backup files may make your information unrecoverable. The app recovery code cannot decrypt a separately exported encrypted file.

6. Ads and privacy choices

In the free version, Google AdMob may serve advertising. Depending on device, consent choices, and Google policies, advertising providers may collect or share IP address, device and advertising identifiers, ad interaction metrics, and diagnostics for delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. Google’s UMP consent platform may present privacy controls.

Recognized MyMoney Pro users do not see advertising banners. This does not prevent independent processing by Google Play or technical purchase verification. MyMoney does not upload the content of your finances, notes, or audio to advertising services.

7. MyMoney Pro purchases and fraud prevention

Google Play processes payments for the permanent MyMoney Pro product. The app does not receive your full payment card number. To check or restore entitlement, the app sends the product identifier, Google Play purchase token, randomly generated request identifier and temporary Play Integrity token over HTTPS to the MyMoney Pro Verifier hosted on Google Cloud.

The server checks ownership, purchase state, and acknowledgement through the Google Play Developer API. It receives no PIN, financial entries, private notes, or voice recordings. Google Play, Play Integrity, and Google Cloud may independently process technical security information, IP addresses, and request logs for operational or anti-fraud purposes.

Entitlement may be rechecked after device changes, refunds, or restoration. Play Integrity checks can limit purchases on uncertified devices or devices that do not meet security requirements.

8. Notifications and permissions

With your authorization, MyMoney schedules local reminders that may show the titles and due dates of bills, notes, or tasks in system notifications. You can disable reminders in MyMoney or Android settings. Exact delivery depends on device permissions and battery restrictions.

Camera, microphone, notifications, biometrics, and one-time file selection permissions are requested only when relevant functions are used. The app does not automatically browse your photo library or files.

9. Reference exchange rates and external services

An optional home-screen card retrieves reference exchange rates from the Frankfurter API. Only the relevant currency codes are sent, not financial balances, account values, or personal notes. The provider may receive technical connection data, such as an IP address.

Links opened voluntarily (YouTube, privacy policy, store, support) and Google Play in-app updates are subject to the independent policies of their providers.

10. Retention and deletion

Your records remain on your device until deleted, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Settings → Clear Data can remove selected categories from the active profile. Previously exported files and copies in third-party services must be removed separately by you.

MyMoney has no developer-managed online user accounts, so it offers neither server-side record syncing nor remote local-data deletion. The developer cannot view, restore, or delete your device-only records remotely. Infrastructure providers may retain limited technical anti-fraud records in accordance with their policies.

11. Children and teens

MyMoney is not specifically directed at children. It should be used in accordance with the age rating displayed on Google Play, with parental guidance where appropriate. It does not provide regulated financial services.

12. Your rights, changes, and contact

You can access, edit, and delete your local entries through the app. For privacy inquiries, applicable data protection rights, or questions about third-party processing, contact us using the email below. We cannot decrypt or recover records that remain solely on your device without access to it.

We may update this policy when features, providers, or legal obligations change. The last-updated date on this page identifies the current revision.
